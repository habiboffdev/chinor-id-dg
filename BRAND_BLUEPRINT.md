# Opportuni — Brand Blueprint

Purpose: a practical, opinionated foundation for a bold, funny, modern, minimal, youth-forward, AI-powered brand. Dark UI with radiant accents. Zero clichés.

## 1) Brand North Star
- Essence: Brave guidance to your next big move.
- One-liner: AI that opens real doors for students.
- Promise: Cut the noise. Surface the right opportunities, faster.
- Proof: Smart matching, verified organizations, clear next steps.

## 2) Positioning
- For: Students and early talents seeking internships, scholarships, competitions, and volunteering.
- Who want: A trusted shortcut to relevant, high-quality opportunities.
- Unlike: Generic job boards and spammy listings.
- We: Use AI + community signals to personalize discovery and remove friction from applying.

## 3) Personality Sliders
- Bold ▮▮▮▮▮▮▮▯▯ Subtle
- Funny ▮▮▮▮▮▯▯▯▯ Serious
- Modern ▮▮▮▮▮▮▮▯▯ Classic
- Minimal ▮▮▮▮▮▮▯▯▯ Ornate
- Warm ▮▮▮▮▯▯▯▯▯ Formal

Tone principles: confident, witty, precise, encouraging. Never cringey or try-hard.

## 4) Messaging Pillars
1) Real growth: actionable opportunities, not endless scrolling.
2) Smarter matching: AI that understands your skills, goals, and timing.
3) Clear guidance: what to do next, why it matters, and how to win.
4) Credible network: verified orgs and transparent requirements.
5) Celebrate momentum: track wins, streaks, and portfolio progress.

## 5) Voice & Style
- Do: punchy sentences, specific benefits, a wink of humor.
- Don’t: buzzword salads, generic inspiration, patronizing advice.
- Lexicon: apply, match, unlock, win, signal, momentum, portfolio, verified, shortlist.
- Microtone: “We got you.” “Let’s ship it.” “One solid application beats ten random ones.”

Examples
- Headline: “Make your move.” / “Apply less. Win more.” / “Your next opportunity is not random.”
- Subhead: “Opportuni uses AI to find roles that actually fit you—then helps you apply like a pro.”
- CTA: “Get matched” / “See your shortlist” / “Start your streak”
- Empty state: “No noise here. Add one skill to unlock matches.”
- Error: “Something tripped. We’re already on it—try again in a sec.”

## 6) Visual Identity

### 6.1 Color System (Dark-first)
- Ink 1000: #070A0F (App background)
- Ink 900: #0B0F14
- Ink 800: #0F1621
- Ink 700: #131B27
- Slate 600: #1A2433 (Elevations, cards)
- Slate 400: #2A3443 (Borders)
- Mist 300: #8FA2B8 (Muted text)
- Mist 200: #B6C3D3 (Secondary text)
- White 0: #FFFFFF (On dark highlights)

Accents (radiant, energetic)
- Neon Lime (Primary): #C8FF00
- Electric Violet (Secondary): #6C63FF
- Cyber Cyan (Tertiary): #00E5FF
- Pulse Coral (Alert/Promo): #FF5C7C

Gradients (sparingly, for hero/cta)
- Aurora 1: linear-gradient(135deg, #6C63FF 0%, #00E5FF 50%, #C8FF00 100%)
- Aurora 2: linear-gradient(135deg, #FF5C7C 0%, #6C63FF 60%)

States
- Success: #1DD1A1
- Warning: #FBC02D
- Danger: #FF4D4F

Contrast: Maintain WCAG AA for text on dark. Prefer Mist 200+ for body text on Ink 800+.

### 6.2 Typography
- Display: Space Grotesk (600/700) — bold, technical, friendly.
- Text: Inter (400/500/600) — highly readable on dark.
- Mono (accents, AI/code moments): JetBrains Mono (400/600).

Type scale
- H1 56/64, H2 40/48, H3 28/36, H4 22/28, Body 16/24, Small 14/20, Micro 12/16.

### 6.3 Layout & Density
- Grid: 12-col, 80–1200px content width, generous negative space.
- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 56, 72.
- Radius: 10px (cards, inputs), 999px (pills).
- Shadows: subtle colored glow on focus/hover using accent hues.

### 6.4 Iconography & Illustration
- Icons: duotone on dark; outline base with accent fill at 10–20%.
- Illustration: abstract constellations, nodes, orbits; no stock photos of handshakes.
- Photography: candid, diverse students in action; real gear, real spaces.

### 6.5 Motion
- Default durations: 120–220ms. Ease-out for entrances, spring/overshoot on hover.
- Hover on CTA: soft outer glow in accent; text shifts +1px for liveliness.

## 7) Logo Directions (conceptual)
Option A — The Portal: a circular “O” aperture with a subtle radiating gradient (Violet→Cyan→Lime). Feels like a gateway.
Option B — The Orbit: stylized O with 3 orbiting dots (skills, goals, timing). Motion-capable.
Option C — The Path: zigzag upward path forming an O, hinting progress without a cliché arrow.

Rules
- Legibility first on dark. Reserve full-bleed Aurora gradients for hero.
- Monochrome marks for small sizes. Safe area: 0.25× mark around.

## 8) Components (UI Kit)
- Navigation: top bar, dark background, active item underlined with accent.
- Buttons:
  - Primary: filled Neon Lime on Ink 900; hover adds Electric Violet glow.
  - Secondary: outline Mist 300; hover fill Ink 700.
  - Tertiary: text button with subtle underline on hover.
- Cards: Ink 800 base, Slate 600 border, 10px radius, faint inner gradient on hover.
- Inputs: Ink 900 fill, Slate 400 border, focus ring in Electric Violet.
- Chips/Tags: pill, minimal; active uses Neon Lime text on Ink 900.
- Badges: small, high-contrast (Success/Warning/Danger palette).
- Empty States: playful icon + one-line quip + clear next step.

## 10) Accessibility
- Minimum AA contrast for all text; prefer AAA for body copy.
- Focus states must be visible without color (outline + offset).
- Avoid gradient-on-gradient for text; gradients for surfaces/accents.
